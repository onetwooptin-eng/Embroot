Delivery & Logistics

How EmberRoot plans biomass deliveries, pallet orders, bulk loads and international enquiries.

Freight is part of the product decision

Biomass is bulky. The best ex-works price may become expensive after handling, pallet weight, border charges and last-mile delivery. We therefore calculate delivery after receiving the destination and order profile.

Information needed

  • Full destination and postal code
  • Product and estimated quantity
  • Bag, pallet, crate, bulk bag or loose-load preference
  • Forklift, loading dock or tail-lift requirement
  • Access limits and delivery time window

International enquiries

Duties, import permits, plant-health rules and local taxes vary. The buyer remains responsible for confirming destination-country requirements unless a written agreement states otherwise.
