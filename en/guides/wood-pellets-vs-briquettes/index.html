Wood Pellets vs Briquettes: Choosing the Right Biomass Fuel

Compare handling, appliances, storage, ash, packaging and delivered cost before choosing pellets or briquettes.

Compare handling, appliances, storage, ash, packaging and delivered cost before choosing pellets or briquettes.

The appliance decides first

Pellets are designed for pellet appliances with controlled feeding. Briquettes are usually handled as larger solid pieces in compatible stoves or boilers. Never choose only by price.

Compare delivered energy, not package price

Moisture, ash, density, fines, package weight and transport all affect value. Request a consistent specification and compare the delivered cost for the usable quantity.

Storage and handling

Both products should be protected from moisture. Pellets can break into fines through rough handling; briquettes can expand or crumble when wet.

This guide is general information. Confirm appliance, legal, import and safety requirements for your exact transaction.
