Understanding ENplus, DINplus and Pellet Claims

How to evaluate certification language, documents, producer identity and batch information.

How to evaluate certification language, documents, producer identity and batch information.

A certification word is not proof

Marketing text can mention a standard without proving that the exact producer and product hold a current certificate. Ask for the certificate number and verify it with the issuing scheme.

Match documents to the offer

Producer name, plant, product grade and packaging identity should align. Be cautious when invoices, bags and certificates show unrelated companies.

Keep batch records

For commercial purchases, retain the specification, certificate, batch information, invoice and delivery evidence.
