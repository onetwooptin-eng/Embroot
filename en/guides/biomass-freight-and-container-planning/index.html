Biomass can cube out before it weighs out

Low-density products may fill a vehicle before reaching its legal payload. Compressed fuels use space differently from loose chips or shavings.

Packaging changes handling cost

Small bags improve retail handling but add packaging and pallet work. Bulk formats can reduce unit cost but require suitable unloading and storage.
