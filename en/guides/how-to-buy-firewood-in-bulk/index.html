Ask how volume is measured

A loose cubic metre, stacked cubic metre and solid cubic metre are not the same. The quotation must state the measurement method.

Moisture and log dimensions

Request an agreed moisture range, log length, split size and tolerance. Confirm whether testing is taken at the surface or after splitting the sample log.

Delivery inspection

Photograph the load, packaging and visible damage. Check quantity, species mix and dimensions promptly against the written order.
